How we adjusted the comps

Three adjustments, each tied to a number

  1. Single-level premium. Boise Hunter's own pricing sets it. The Cottonwood at 21852 Appaloosa lists at $361 per sq ft, while the two-story Tablerock at 21726 Appaloosa lists at $343. That $18 per sq ft gap is about $58,000 on 3,227 sq ft, applied to the two-story comps.
  2. Size. About $150 per sq ft of difference from your 3,227 sq ft.
  3. Market timing. 6837 Campolina went under contract in February, when rates ran about 1.5 points lower than today's 7.5%. A 3% market-conditions adjustment accounts for that. Star's sold price per sq ft held flat over the year ($280 to $282), which keeps the adjustment modest.

Bedroom and bath count deductions from the RPR report are removed because they double-count square footage.
