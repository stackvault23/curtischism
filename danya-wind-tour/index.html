Danya Wind — Weekend Driving Tour

Eagle, downtown Boise, Barber / East Boise and Meridian, laid out as two easy loops instead of a zigzag across the valley.

Hi Danya,

I put together a simple way to spend the weekend so you can get a feel for the areas without zigzagging all over the valley. Both days start from the Hilton Garden Inn in Eagle (I'm not sure where you ended up staying, but I think that's the one I had suggested). If you're somewhere else, the routes still work in the same order.

Saturday gives you Eagle's lifestyle, downtown Boise and every Barber option in one eastbound loop. Sunday works north to south through Meridian. I included the parks, restaurants and shopping you'd likely use day to day along with the homes. Tap Zillow for photos and details, Directions to navigate, and check off homes as you drive by. Your check-marks, thumbs and notes stay saved on this phone.

— Curtis
